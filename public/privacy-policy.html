Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, account login details, order history, payment reference, and the player or server details needed to deliver a game top-up.

  • To process orders, verify payments, and deliver digital products.
  • To provide customer support and resolve complaints.

Service providers

Paystack processes payments. Our game-top-up fulfillment provider may receive the player and server details required to complete an order. Email and hosting providers may process information needed for account, security, and support operations. Hiro Official Store does not store your full card details.

Storage and security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account and order information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to information we must retain for completed transactions, fraud prevention, or legal requirements.

Children

The store is intended for customers aged 18 and above. If you believe a minor has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it.
